# Bulldogs Card Picker

Sports card and poster picker for Bulldogs families: 61 trading cards (37 soccer, 12 baseball, 12 football) and 15 cut-out composite posters (5 per sport). Each sport shows one lineup, cards first and posters after (soccer #38-42, baseball and football #13-17, gold Poster tag). Pick a sport, pick a design, fill in the player details, and send the request.

Features: holographic hero card, 3D tilt on hover, style filters and search, "Surprise me", shortlist (saved in the browser), lightbox with keyboard and swipe, live preview with the player's name, confetti on send.

Deep links: `#soccer`, `#baseball`, `#football`; add `-posters` (e.g. `#soccer-posters`) to open with the Posters filter on.

Images: `img/t*.jpg` soccer cards, `img/b*.jpg` baseball, `img/f*.jpg` football, `img/ps*.jpg` / `pb*.jpg` / `pf*.jpg` posters (1200px). Grid thumbnails live in `img/thumb/`. `img/placeholder/` holds the blank silhouettes used in the Canva poster templates.
